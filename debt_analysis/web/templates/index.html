Distress signals

    Liquid assets vs. total debt

    Cash + short-term investments against funded debt + finance leases.

    Capital structure

    Debt due within 12 months, long-term debt (incl. finance leases), and operating lease liabilities.

    Current & quick ratio

    Quick = (cash + short-term investments + receivables) / current liabilities. Reference line at 1.0×.

    Quarterly free cash flow

    Operating cash flow − capex, de-accumulated from year-to-date 10-Q statements.

    Shareholder returns vs. free cash flow (TTM)

    Buybacks + dividends funded beyond free cash flow come straight out of liquidity.

    EBITDA vs. interest (TTM)

    GAAP EBITDA = operating income + D&A. Adjusted adds back impairments.

    Leverage multiples

    Gaps mean EBITDA was ≤ 0, so the multiple is undefined. Axis capped at 20×; the Data tab has exact values.

    Notes: carrying principal vs. fair value

    Fair value from the debt footnote. The ratio is the market price in cents on the dollar. Before 2020, principal ≈ non-current debt.

    Altman Z'' score

    Non-manufacturing version. Below 1.1 = distress, 1.1–2.6 = grey, above 2.6 = safe.
